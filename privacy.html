Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

Silence Timer is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal personal data necessary to provide our services. This may include your email address if you join our testing waitlists, and anonymous crash reports to help us improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Notify you about app updates and testing availability.
  • Improve the functionality and stability of Silence Timer.
  • Provide customer support.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your local timer settings and presets are stored directly on your device. Any data transmitted to our servers (like crash reports or email waitlist signups) is secured using industry-standard encryption.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via our Support page.
