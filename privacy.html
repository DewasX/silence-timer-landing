Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At Silence Timer, we believe your focus habits are your business. Our application is built with a fundamentally "offline-first" approach, meaning the vast majority of our features require absolutely zero data transmission to our servers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Silence Timer does not collect, track, or transmit to external servers any of the following:

  • Your daily, weekly, or lifetime focus analytics (Total Sessions, Focus Time).
  • Your custom timer presets (Names, durations, colors, icons).
  • The content of your device's silenced notifications.

All of the above data remains strictly isolated and securely stored in the local SQLite database directly on your mobile device.

3. Information We May Collect

We only process data when explicitly voluntarily provided by you, or automatically collected by trusted third-party distribution platforms:

  • Email Communications: If you utilize our Support forms or join an iOS testing Waitlist, we collect your email address and message contents solely to respond to your inquiry or invite you to the app tests. (These forms are powered by third-party services like Formspree).
  • Analytics & Crash Logs: If you opt-in to anonymous analytics on your device via Apple App Store or Google Play Store, we may receive aggregate, anonymized crash reports and generic usage statistics provided by those platforms. This helps us squash bugs and improve stability.

4. Third-Party Services

The App and Landing Page utilize specific third-party services that may have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • Google Play Store / Apple App Store / TestFlight: For app distribution and anonymous crash reporting.
  • Formspree: For securely handling emails submitted through our website's communication forms.
  • GitHub Pages: For hosting this informational landing page.

5. System Permissions

To function correctly, Silence Timer requests access to OS-level system permissions, such as "Do Not Disturb" access. This permission is utilized exclusively locally on your device to silence notifications during active timers. The app does not read your notifications or transmit them off-device.

6. Data Security & Retention

Emails collected for support inquiries or waitlists will be retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the communication's purpose. Because core app data is stored locally, uninstalling Silence Timer from your device will permanently delete your stored timer analytics and preset configurations.

7. Updates to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document or via in-app notifications.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related concerns or data inquiries, please use our secure Support page.
