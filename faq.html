Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the Android early testing?

Due to Google Play's 20-tester requirement, you must first join our Google Group to be whitelisted. Once joined, the download link will work for your account on the Play Store.

When is the iOS version coming out?

We are actively developing the iOS version. Please click the "iOS App Coming Soon" button on the home page and provide your email to be notified the moment TestFlight spots open up!

Does Silence Timer require an internet connection?

No! Silence Timer is designed to work completely offline so you can disconnect and truly focus. Internet is only required to download the app and fetch occasional updates.

How does Strict Silence Mode work?

Strict Silence Mode integrates deeply with your phone's native Do Not Disturb APIs. When initiated, it mutes incoming notifications based on your strictness settings until the timer ends.

Is my focus data private?

Yes. All of your focus sessions, analytics, and custom presets are stored securely and locally on your phone database. We do not sell or upload your focus data.
